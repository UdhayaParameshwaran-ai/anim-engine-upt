Text Motion — 8 animation styles, 4 themes

Each variation is a different way the text itself animates: pop, fade, typing, decode, masked rise, tracking, flip, karaoke. What they share: every line has a weight (weak lines are small captions, the strong line is about seven times bigger and gradient-filled, numbers count up), the timing follows the narration speed in words per minute, scenes change with a new tint and their own transition, and all colours come from the theme. Open any one to edit the lines, mark accent words with *asterisks*, pin lines to voice-over cues and change the speed. Pick a theme below: it restyles every preview.

01 · Beats

variant="beats"

Pop: each word springs in with overshoot and a short vertical blur, on the beat of the voice. Strong words punch the frame; scenes change with a wipe.

02 · Fade

variant="fade"

Words dissolve in out of soft focus while drifting gently upward, slower for heavier lines. Calm and cinematic; scenes cross-dissolve.

03 · Typewriter

variant="typewriter"

Characters are typed one by one at the speed of the narration, each flashing the accent colour, with a caret that blinks in the pauses.

04 · Decode

variant="decode"

Each letter cycles through random glyphs before it locks onto the right character, left to right as the word is spoken.

05 · Rise

variant="rise"

Words slide up from behind an invisible edge with a slight tilt that straightens as they land. No fading: sharp and edited.

06 · Tracking

variant="tracking"

Letters start spread wide and blurred, then draw in together into the word. The strong line is pulled into focus.

07 · Flip

variant="flip"

Each word swings up on a hinge in 3D with a small overshoot; strong words turn on their vertical axis. Scenes turn like a page.

08 · Karaoke

variant="karaoke"

The whole sentence is there, dimmed; each word lights up exactly as it is spoken, with a soft highlight on the current word.
