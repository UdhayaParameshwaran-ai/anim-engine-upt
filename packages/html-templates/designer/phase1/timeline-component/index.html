Timeline — 3 layouts, 4 themes

Built from the real Timeline component. The Ribbon is a copy of its sizes, strokes, timings and theme looks; Camera Track and Filmstrip Focus are the two other layouts. Choose where the cards come in from and the order they arrive in (Ribbon), and the pace (all three). Ribbon cards can hold a picture, a sentence or a stat. Bar, line, donut and gauge cards, and the narration-driven highlight, are in the component only. Pick a theme below: it restyles every preview.
