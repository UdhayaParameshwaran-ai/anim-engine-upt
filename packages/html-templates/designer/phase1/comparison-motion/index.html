Comparison: 5 motion ideas, 4 themes

The same before-and-after content, shown five ways. The first item holds the two side names (left / right), and every item after that is one row. The right side is treated as the better one. Pick a theme below to restyle every preview.

01 · Split Wipe

New motion

A slider handle sweeps in and splits the frame into before and after. Each row then wipes in on both sides while the handle nudges.

02 · Verdict Columns

New motion

Two cards side by side. Each row lands as a pair and gets its verdict: a drawn cross on the left, a tick with a ripple on the right.

03 · Balance

New motion

A beam scale. For each row, a block drops into each pan; the better side keeps winning, so the beam tips further and wobbles as it settles.

04 · Flip Cards

New motion

Each card shows the problem. On its beat it flips over in 3D, lifting as it turns, to show the solution on its back.

05 · Versus

New motion

The two sides charge in and collide in the middle with a flash, a shockwave and a VS badge. They recoil apart, and each row is called out on alternating sides.
