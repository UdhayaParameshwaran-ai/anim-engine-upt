Scene Transitions — 10 ways one scene gives way to the next, 4 themes

Every variation plays the same two scenes, so the difference you see is the transition itself. While it runs, the content inside each scene moves at its own speed (parallax), so the change has depth instead of being a flat cut; when the new scene lands, its title words rise into place. Open any one to edit both scenes' titles, subtitles and tints, choose the direction (for the directional ones) and set the pace. Wrap a word in *asterisks* for the accent colour. All colours and fonts come from the theme. Pick a theme below: it restyles every preview.

01 · Wipe

transition="wipe"

A soft-edged line uncovers the new scene with a thin light leading it, while old and new content slide in opposite senses. Directional.

02 · Push

transition="push"

The new scene pushes the old one out; the old moves less and dims and shrinks, so the scenes sit at different depths. Directional.

03 · Zoom through

transition="zoom"

The camera flies into the old scene until it dissolves into blur, and the new scene grows out of the distance into focus.

04 · Iris

transition="iris"

A circle opens from the centre and the new scene fills it, with a thin ring on its edge. The old scene eases back.

05 · Slats

transition="slats"

The new scene arrives in eight strips that open one after another, alternating up and down. Directional.

06 · Split

transition="split"

The old scene parts down the middle like doors to uncover the new one, which settles in from slightly larger. Directional.

07 · Defocus

transition="defocus"

The old scene drifts out of focus and fades while the new one pulls into focus, with a soft bloom. Calm and cinematic.

08 · Cube

transition="cube"

The scenes are faces of a cube that turns in 3D; the new face swings to the front. Directional.

09 · Panel

transition="panel"

A block of theme colour sweeps across and covers the frame; the scene changes behind it and the block sweeps away. Directional.

10 · Ripple

transition="ripple"

The old scene dissolves into the new through a wave of distortion that swells in the middle and calms again.
