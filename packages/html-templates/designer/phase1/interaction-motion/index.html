Interaction kit: 5 motion ideas, 4 themes

How a person uses the product: click, drag, type and send, toggle, and touch. Each item is one action: its label, the text it types or shows, and an icon. Pick a theme below to restyle every preview.

01 · Cursor & Click

New motion

The cursor glides to each button on a natural arc. The button lifts on hover, presses with a ripple from the click point, and turns on with a drawn check.

cursor glidepress ripplehover lift

02 · Drag & Drop

New motion

The cursor grabs a card. It lifts and tilts with its own speed while carried, the board glows as it arrives, and it settles into its slot with a bounce.

velocity tiltlift shadowdrop glow

03 · Prompt & Send

New motion

The request is typed with a caret and the cursor clicks send. The text lifts out of the box into a sent bubble, the chat scrolls up, and a reply resolves from typing dots.

typing caretsend liftreply dots

04 · Toggle Switches

New motion

The cursor flips each setting. The knob stretches as it slides and snaps round, the track fills with colour, and the row wipes in its new state.

knob squashtrack fillstate wipe

05 · Touch & Swipe

New motion

On a phone, a fingertip presses with a ripple and drags the carousel. When it lets go, the cards carry on with momentum and spring onto the next page.

finger ripplemomentumspring snap
