Process steps: 9 motion ideas, 4 themes

Each variant walks through the same steps with its own motion idea: a road, a deck, a reel, a ring, a tower and a camera dolly. They all follow the same rules: the current step moves, finished steps step back, text blur-rises word by word, and each motion ends where the next step appears. Pace sets the gap and the hold. Pick a theme below to restyle every preview.

01 · Morph Relay

New motion

One shape carries the whole process. At each step it morphs into a new form, turning and squashing as it changes and taking the step colour while its icon swaps. Every finished form is stamped into a row below.

02 · Card Deck

New motion

Each step is a card on a deck. When it's done, the card lifts, tips back in 3D and arcs into a "done" tray, and the deck slides forward to the next card.

3D flipdone traydepth stack

03 · Accordion Focus

New motion

A highlight slides to the current row with a springy overshoot and the row opens to reveal its detail, while the previous row folds shut and gets a drawn check. The list reflows smoothly as rows open and close.

04 · Orbit

New motion

The steps sit on a tilted 3D ring that turns to bring each one to the front. The front step is large, sharp and lit; the back ones are small and soft. Dots travel round the ring.

3D carouseldepth of fieldcyclic feel

05 · Block Stack

New motion

Each step drops onto the last: it falls under gravity, squashes and springs back, the tower shudders and dust puffs out. Then a leader line draws out to its detail.

physicssquash & stretchbuilds up

06 · Domino Chain

New motion

Cause and effect. Each tile is knocked and teeters while its step is explained, then falls into the next tile, which is the next step. The last one lands flat with a bounce.

cause → effectgravityheld tension

07 · Path Walker

New motion

A glowing traveller walks a winding road. The road ahead is a faint dashed line and turns solid behind it. Each step blooms on arrival: the disc lights, the icon draws, a ring pulses and the text flips in.

08 · Dolly Pan

New motion

The steps sit on a set wider than the screen. The camera dollies from one to the next with parallax numerals and specks, and the frame softens with the speed of the move. Neighbours peek in at the edges, and the text slides in with the camera.

09 · Number Rail

New motion

Editorial type. A giant numeral rolls like a reel to the next step, with its neighbours faint above and below. A progress rail fills, and the title and detail shear into place word by word.
