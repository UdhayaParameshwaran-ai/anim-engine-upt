Titles and Openers — 14 ways to open a scene, 4 themes

Variations 1–6 are fuller title scenes (kicker, title, subtitle, byline); 7–14 are the calm set, with one focal point (the title) and a quiet subtitle and nothing else. Every variation is a different way a title scene opens, and they share the same content, so the difference you see is the motion. Wrap a word in *asterisks* for the accent colour. Pace stretches or tightens the intro, hold and play-out; Play out adds the exit so the title leaves cleanly into the next scene. All colours and fonts come from the theme. Pick a theme below: it restyles every preview.

01 · Rise

full title scene

Kicker pops, title words slide up from behind an invisible edge, an accent rule draws, then subtitle and byline settle in. The clean default opener.

02 · Curtain

full title scene

Two panels part to reveal the title while its letters draw together from wide spacing. A theatrical opener.

03 · Logo reveal

full title scene

A ring draws itself, the disc pops, the icon strokes in under a light sweep, then the name unfolds beside it.

04 · Shine

full title scene

The title resolves from a soft blur while a band of light sweeps across it, with a slow glow pulse. Premium and calm.

05 · Card

full title scene

A title card tilts up in 3D with an icon disc and a progress line that fills across the hold. A chapter or episode card.

06 · Cinema

full title scene

Letterbox bars slide in, the title tracks together slowly under a push-in, a thin rule opens and the subtitle fades beneath.

07 · Beam

calm set · title + subtitle only

A soft band of light travels across and the title is left sharp behind it, as if the light were writing it.

08 · Weight wave

calm set · title + subtitle only

Letters start as hairlines and swell to full weight in a wave. Pure typography, nothing else moves.

09 · Elastic

calm set · title + subtitle only

Letters snap in from tall and thin, springing out from the centre of the title, and settle with a tiny wobble.

10 · Depth

calm set · title + subtitle only

Each word arrives from far behind the screen, out of focus; earlier words ease back so the frame has depth.

11 · Reel

calm set · title + subtitle only

Every letter is a small mechanical reel that spins through other characters and slows to land on the right one.

12 · Liquid

calm set · title + subtitle only

The title forms out of a rippling distortion that calms into crisp letters, like surfacing through water.

13 · Spotlight

calm set · title + subtitle only

A pool of light travels along the dark title and leaves it lit behind it, then opens over the whole line.

14 · Convergence

calm set · title + subtitle only

Three colour layers drift apart and slide together into one sharp title, like a print coming into register.
