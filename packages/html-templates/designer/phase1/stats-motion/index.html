Stats: 7 motion ideas, 4 themes

Each variant tells the same numbers with its own motion idea: reels, units, liquid, depth, area and a tracked line. They all follow the same rules: one moving thing at a time, blur-rise text, a slow drift during holds, and the next element appearing where the eye already is. Pace sets the gap and the hold. Pick a theme below to restyle every preview.

01 · Tape Measure

New motion

The value is read off a ruler. The tape scrolls under a fixed needle and locks on, with blur following its speed. The distance travelled is the change: a band spans from the last flag to the needle, and every stat leaves a flag behind.

measurementdistance = changeflags

02 · Unit Swarm

New motion

The number is made of 100 units. Dots fly out of a drifting cloud on curved paths and lock into a 10×10 grid. Dots added this beat glow and older ones settle back, so the growth shows without reading.

visual metaphorparticles1 dot = unit

03 · Liquid Tubes

New motion

Glass tubes fill with liquid that sloshes and settles while bubbles rise. The value floats on the surface. Focus walks from tube to tube with a camera lean, then every tube returns and the highlight glows.

slosh physicsfocus walkcamera lean

04 · Bubble Pack

New motion

Each stat inflates as a bubble whose area is the value. It floats into the cluster and shoulders the others aside with real collision physics, then everything settles with a soft wobble. The newest bubble is lit.

area = valuecollision physicssoft wobble

05 · Scale Zoom

New motion

Each value is a square whose area is the number, all growing from one corner. The newest square always fills the frame: the camera pulls back and older squares shrink inside it. The panel shows ×-times vs the first.

area = valuecamera pull-back× ratio

06 · Comet Trail

New motion

A glowing comet draws the line, and the number riding on it is the value of the line under it, so it flows between points. A level line reads its height off the axis, and pins drop in at each point.

live valueaxis read-offpin drop

07 · Odometer

New motion

Each digit is a mechanical reel. The ones digit spins most, like a real counter, and motion blur follows each reel's speed. The label flips down like a counter wheel, and finished numbers drop into a history rail.

digit reelsspeed blurhistory rail
