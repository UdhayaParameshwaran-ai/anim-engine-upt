    Every template is a frame, a grid and a few slots.

    This page is the rulebook for building templates: what the page frame allows, which slot layouts are approved, what can go in each slot, and how to check a template before it is made.

    What is new in v2

    01 · Frame

    The page frame and its six sizes

    A template has up to three zones. Only the content zone is required. Turning the header or footer on or off changes only the height of the content area, giving six modes.

    • Header holds the title and an optional subtitle. Three options: none, title only, title and subtitle.
    • Content holds 1 to 4 slots on a 12-column grid. Margin 96 px, gap 32 px.
    • Footer holds the brand mark, a note and the page number. Two options: off or on.
    • Missing zones collapse. The content area grows into the freed space. There are never empty bands.

    04 · Slot shapes

    Five slot shapes, one image ratio each

    There are many slot sizes but only five shapes. Prepare one asset per shape instead of one per slot. Always supply the asset 20% larger than the slot, with the subject in the middle 60%.

      07 · Growth

      27 approved, about 220 possible

      The gap is always 32 px and a template has at most 4 slots, so the only thing that can grow is where the cuts fall on the grid. The 27 layouts are a chosen set, not a limit.

      Counts assume cuts at columns 3 to 9 or at 1/3, 1/2, 2/3 of the height, with each slot at least 3 columns wide (408 px) and 150 px tall. They are an estimate. Most of the unapproved ones are awkward, with very uneven or thin slots.

      Test for adding a new layout

      1. It has 1 to 4 slots.
      2. Every vertical cut is on the 12-column grid. Every horizontal cut is at 1/3, 1/2 or 2/3.
      3. Every slot is at least 408 px wide and 150 px tall.
      4. It passes rules R3 to R8 with a realistic set of elements.
      5. It gets the next free code L{n}-{nn}, a card in the layout section and a row in the JSON file.
